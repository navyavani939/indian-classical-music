About Indian Classical Music

Indian Classical Music is one of the oldest musical traditions in the world. It represents India's culture, heritage and spirituality.

Hindustani Music

Hindustani Classical Music is mainly practiced in North India. It focuses on ragas, talas and improvisation.

Carnatic Music

Carnatic Classical Music is mainly practiced in South India. It is known for its rich compositions and melodies.
